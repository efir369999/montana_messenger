# The findings this tree carries

The harness compares what has a counterpart: every value of the Decree, every domain, every frozen
vector of the set, every numeral the sources declare and every layout they hold, against the code
that answers for it, on every build. What has no counterpart it does not compare, and therefore
does not catch — a derive on a type, a feature of a dependency, a door left wide — because these
are not values and there is nothing on the other side to hold them against. A finding of that
class, written nowhere, lives in whoever found it and goes with the session that found it.

So it lives here. Each entry names where it is, what it is, why it is a finding rather than a
preference, and what closes it. A finding closes by construction or it does not close; a finding
argued away leaves its entry standing with the argument written in it, and a closed one is removed
with the commit that closes it.

Nothing here is scheduled by this file. What the plan already owns is listed at the end so it is
not filed twice, and what the plan does not yet own is marked as exactly that.

## What the gate does not compare

**One thing of this tree stands here, and it is the gate's own denominator.** The rest of the
findings this section carried are closed by construction, and what closed each of them is written
below rather than remembered. The questions that remain beyond the one below are not this tree's to
answer: they belong to the set, and they stand in the last section with the item of the plan that
owns them.

**The numerals the gate counts are declarations, and a number inside a function is invisible to
it.** The pass that refuses a numeral standing in no register reads lines that declare a constant;
a literal written inside the body of a function is a number it never sees. That is exactly the shape
the modulus of the lattice had when it was found — a literal inside a function of the circuit, while
the same number stood in a register — and moving that one number closed the instance and not the
class. What holds the class today is a reading rather than a mechanism: the bodies of the library
were walked and carry twenty-four literals of three digits or more, every one of them structural (a
table of two hundred fifty-six entries, a width of a format, the constant of a mixing that is work
and not a value) or a row of the Decree itself, which the gate compares row by row from the other
side. Closing it by construction means teaching the pass to read expressions rather than
declarations and to tell a value of the set from the size of an array — which is a pass worth
writing and is not written.

This sentence was false once and it is worth keeping the reason. A pass over the twelve closed
stages found seven things standing here while the line above said none did, and every one of them
was of exactly the class this section is for: a place where the promise "a divergence fails the
build" was held by the care of whoever wrote it rather than by a mechanism. None of the seven
reached a running path — but a section claiming to be empty is a section nobody reads, and that
is what let them stand. What closed each is written below.

**A beacon does not fit a cell, and the key it is sealed under is the point it stands at.** Everything
that crosses this wire is a cell of one width, and a beacon is five times that: six thousand one
hundred and eighty-nine bytes against one thousand two hundred and thirty-two, because it carries
the cement state whole. So a beacon travels as a delivery of several cells, exactly as any body
larger than a cell does. What was missing was the key those cells are sealed under, and the set
already held the rule that answers it: the key of a pipe is derived from the value that carries the
right to read what travels in it. For a letter that value is the secret two correspondents derive;
for an object of consensus it is the point it stands at, which every machine computes from the
window, the chain, the round and the index without asking anybody. The set now states the whole of
it — a cell standing at a point takes **both** its keys from that point, its label is the point, and
`target_commit` carries the point rather than a machine — so a publication keeps the one shape every
cell has and a beacon and a letter are indistinguishable on the wire. Nothing is weakened by keys
everyone computes: what stands at a point is for everyone by construction. Closed: `mt-wire`'s
`publish` cuts an object into cells under those keys and reads it back, a scenario of two machines
carries a beacon of six thousand bytes across and the far side parses it whole, a point of another
round answers with nothing, and the two derivations are frozen in the Canon and compared by the
gate.

**A runner does not read the cement; it attempts the close, and the circuit answers.** A window
closes when the cement opens to a sum of distinct standing meeting the quorum share of the committed
total. Both quantities are commitments — the cement is a sum of the commitments the attestations
carried, and opening it takes the blinding factor of every machine that entered it — so no machine
but their owners can open either, and a runner is not among them. What was read as a missing rule
was the wrong question: a runner never decides the close and cannot. It **attempts**. It builds the
proof of the window over what it has gathered, and the circuit asserts the cement against the quorum
share of the committed total; a window below the share yields no proof, because the assertion is
false and a false statement does not prove, and a window at or above it yields one — and that proof
**is** the close. So the decision is never an observation and never a judgement, and two runners of
one chain reach the same answer because they attempt the same statement. What a runner reads to know
when to attempt is public and names nobody: how many part-nullifiers its cement holds, and how many
machines the tree of the admitted carries. Below the share the attempt is certain to fail and is not
made; at or above it the attempt is made. Counting is not the rule and never becomes it. Closed: the
set states it in Consensus, and `Pulse::attempts_the_close` reads the two counts and nothing else,
with the scenario of two machines holding it to both sides of the share.

**A body of several groups was read back with the parity spliced into it, and the first vector of
the door did not catch it.** The delivery that carries an object larger than a cell cuts it into
blocks, groups them `erasure_data` at a time and answers `erasure_group` cells per group, of which
the last quarter is parity. The door that reads an object back placed every block by the index it
carried and concatenated them in that order — which is the cells in order and not the data of the
groups in order, so the parity of one group stood between the data of it and the data of the next.
A beacon stands in one group, and in one group the two readings agree byte for byte; the first
vector of this door carried a beacon-sized body, so it passed while measuring nothing. What found it
was a frame — two hundred eleven thousand bytes, twenty-one groups — which came back as noise and
parsed as nothing. Closed: the door reconstructs each group through `erasure::reconstruct`, which is
where the code lives, and the vectors now carry a body of three groups and a group robbed of exactly
its parity's worth and of one cell past it. It is the class the role names: a vector standing on a
degenerate input is a vector that proves the code computes something rather than the right thing,
and the wrong implementation it must name was, here, "concatenate the cells in the order they came".

**What is owed is not a proof but a division, and that is the eleventh.** The set says the argument

**The first route is extraction**, the one every folding scheme takes: from two accepting answers to
two challenges, build the witnesses of both folded claims. One step of it is sound and cheap here —
the challenge is a bit, so the two answers subtract and nothing is divided. But a chain composes
extractors, and composing them costs two to the depth: at eight windows two hundred and fifty-six
runs, at a hundred and twenty-eight windows the security target itself, at one period of the set two
to the twenty thousandth. **A chain of windows has no bound on its length**, so this route does not
reach the end of it — not because the proof is unwritten but because the composition is exponential
in the very quantity Montana leaves unbounded.

**The second route is a union bound and needs no extractor at all.** If a folded claim is false, a
random bit carries the falseness through with probability one half, so a hundred and twenty-eight
bits in parallel let a false claim survive one fold with probability two to the minus hundred and
twenty-eight; over a period of the set that is below two to the minus hundred and thirteen, over a
billion windows below two to the minus ninety-eight. The chain costs a factor of its length and
nothing more. **But this route buys its cheapness with a condition: the accumulated relation must be
linear over the commitment**, since only then does the falseness of a part carry into the
combination by the algebra alone.

**And the assertions of a window are not linear.** A threshold is an inequality, a range is a
decomposition into bits, and a bit is quadratic. So the second route does not apply to the window's
statement as the set writes it, and the first does not reach the end of the chain. What the set owes
is therefore **a division of the window's statement into the part that accumulates and the part that
does not** — the linear part carried by the fold and answered by the union bound, the non-linear
part answered where it stands. Until that division is written, the two routes are named and neither
is taken, and calling the remainder "a proof yet to be written" would be the same error as calling a
scalar challenge cheap: the true statement is that the shape is not yet the shape a proof can be

## The hardening of the Kernel

The stage of the layers gave this tree thirteen items: six that harden the Kernel where it is
written, and seven that grow the gate. All thirteen stand, and what each is held by is written where
it is stated rather than remembered. Under them all lies one property — that the map from an object

**H-1, item 29 — closed.** The alphabet is held by the type: `Domain` sits behind a private module
and is minted only by a `const fn` of the crate, which refuses a byte outside the alphabet where a
name is written. A name carrying one no longer compiles.

**H-3, item 31 — closed.** The empty leaf is the leaf domain of its construction over a body of no

## The frame these were found under

A finding of the Kernel is not found by computing; it is found by reading, and the reading has a
test. **Replace the hash with an ideal oracle and see whether the attack survives.** If it
survives, the defect is of the grammar and no primitive repairs it; if it dies, it was never of the
Kernel at all.

Under that test the health of the Kernel is one property: the map from an object to its preimage is
injective. Everything above that is grammar reduces to it — that a class is recoverable from a
preimage, that a body parses one way, that a value has one encoding, that an identifier is taken
over the scope of its own object, that two states do not share a root. Where injectivity holds,
every attack of the class becomes a collision in a function, which is the concern of a row of a
table and has a procedure of retirement. Where it does not, a perfect function does not repair it.

The second half of the frame is the demand table the stage of the layers calls for: each form of
the Kernel names the property it asks of the function — collision, preimage, pseudorandomness,
width, and provability inside the circuit. With it, admitting a row is a check. Without it, the
door is an argument.
