# The plan, in twenty-one stages

An empty directory at the start. At the end: a network a stranger joins by verifying one proof, in
which a person reaches another by a name and nobody else learns of it, a body is kept alive by
whoever loves it and found by whoever seeks it, and a client written by someone else stands on the
same network rather than on one that resembles it. Nothing stubbed, nothing refused by design — the
whole protocol working.

Each stage names what it holds, what proves it, and the one condition under which it is done. None
of them closes on "it compiles": each closes against something frozen in `../docs`, and the stage
after it is checkable only because the one before it is.

## What this has to be, and where each of it is answered

A network of this kind differs from a program that works, and the difference is a list rather than
a feeling. Twelve properties, each of which a stranger can test, and beside each the stage that owes
it. A plan is complete when every row has a stage, and a row without one is work nobody planned. The
converse does not hold and should not: nine of these stages stand in no row, and they are the
material the rest are made of — the byte layer, the trees, the birth of a person, the objects, the
suite, the wire, the pulse, the proof machinery and the node that runs. A row reaches them through
the stage that owes it.

Twelve, and two of them pull against each other on purpose, and the tension is the design rather
than a defect: entry asks nobody, and entry is not free. What it costs is one corrupted person of
the fabric per rate of newcomers, and nothing cheaper — not compute, not money, not wall-clock.

## What can be begun today, and what waits on what

The order above is the order of dependency, and a reader entitled to start work is entitled to
know which door is open now. Twelve stages are closed and their code stands. The eleventh closed
on its own condition: a real frame — six redemptions, one of them a right — is proven, verified
and read back as bytes at the shape the Decree names; a frame that spends what it does not hold,
a right taken at a moment its delay does not name, and a share claimed by an invented secret are
refused; the length is the one the set derives; and the peak memory of proving stands measured at
two hundred ninety-five mebibytes against the half-gigabyte budget the parameters were derived
from. The twelfth closed on its own: the three descriptions are proven end to end and their identifiers
frozen as the Decree's `air_hash`, the Genesis State Hash exists, and a population that loses more
than the complement of the quorum resumes by the rule alone. What is open now is the thirteenth —
two hosts, a network and two wallets — and beside it the half of the
seventeenth stage that stands on the wire alone: the
machine holding a tag found, a cell carried over hops to a machine no hop is connected to, the
deferred collection, the waking of a sleeping device, the stream and the budget spent on a
stranger. That stage cannot close until a name can be claimed, but the work of it can begin today
and it is not small.

Everything else waits, and it is honest to say through what. The items of the ninth stage that
change the set travel as one proposal of the constitutional lifecycle; the two the tenth stage
stood on — whether the function under the domains is a row of a table, and whether the length of a
proof is a constant or a declaration of an artifact — are settled in the set, because a circuit
binds both inside itself and a circuit rebuilt is the most expensive object this plan holds. From
the tenth the chain runs
unbroken: the circuits, the values that return to the documents, the node, the join, the crossing,
the planes, the reach, the population, the library, the bindings, and the observer.

**The two the tenth stage waited on stand.** The function under the domains is a row of the hash
table, and the length of a proof is a declaration of its artifact, derived where the contents of a
proof are fixed — so the circuit can be written knowing what it binds, and the most expensive object
this plan holds is not built twice. This tree's own thirteen stand with them: the six that harden
the Kernel and the seven that grow the gate, and the ledger of what the gate does not compare is
empty of this tree. **The twelfth stage is closed, and the thirteenth is what stands open.**

What still travels as one proposal are the items that move no byte of any object — the readings the
set gives twice, the shape of a preimage as a column of the registry, the names of two quantities —
and the stages that wait on those wait on them by name rather than through the tenth.

**The harness is not a stage.** It exists from the second stage and grows with every one after: the
values of the Genesis Decree, the sizes, the domains and the frozen vectors are parsed out of the
set and asserted against the code on every build. A stage is done when its part of the harness is
green, and no stage is done twice.

**The weakest device the protocol claims is an input of every construction, never a test of one.**
A telephone is what this network is built for, so the budget of a telephone — its memory, its
battery and what it can carry in a window — stands **before** the parameters of a mechanism and
derives them. A quantity chosen first and measured afterwards is a quantity that can fail the
measurement, and a mechanism that can fail it is a mechanism already written wrongly: the failure
is of the design and never of the device. No stage of this plan closes on "it fitted"; a stage
closes on the derivation that made fitting the only possible outcome, with the measurement standing
beside it as a witness rather than as a verdict.

**A node is any device reachable from outside.** A telephone, a tablet, a desktop, a server, a
set-top box, a refrigerator — what makes one is that it answers at its own address and does the
work of a machine, and nothing else does. No stage claims a class of device, no quantity of the
set counts them, and no ceiling stands over their number: what bounds a device is what that device
can carry, which is a row of a form and moves at a boundary, and never a row of the block a chain
opens on.

**Every capability stays where it is exercised.** A key is drawn on the machine from its own
entropy and never leaves it; what is stored is stored locally; coordination runs to points every
machine computes alone. No stage introduces a party holding a capability for another, and a node on
a server is the same node run from a terminal.

## Stage 1 — the workspace and the Decree

**Holds.** The workspace, the pinned toolchain, the build-and-test bound — two build tasks and
one test thread at a time — and one crate
holding the parameters of the Genesis Decree and nothing else. Every other crate reads them from
there and none restates a value.

**Done when.** A parameter exists in exactly one place in the tree, and a search for any of its
numbers outside that place returns nothing.

## Stage 2 — the hash primitive, the encoding, and the harness

**Holds.** The domain-separated primitive with its NUL, the registry of separators, unsigned
little-endian encoding in declaration order, the canonical orders, and the round trip in both
directions for every class. With them the harness itself: the parser of the set and the gate that
fails a build on divergence.

**Done when.** The vectors of the primitive reproduce, the bijection holds under a randomized round
trip, and changing a value in the documents breaks the build with that value named.

## Stage 3 — the two trees and the fabric

**Holds.** The sparse tree keyed by a value, the append-only tree indexed by position, the fabric of
time under its own domains, their empty values, and both inclusion proofs.

**Done when.** Every frozen empty value, root and pinned walk reproduces — the walk vectors stand
away from position zero, where a wrong bit order shows — and a proof recomputes a root rather than
trusting the one it carries.

## Stage 4 — the birth of a person

**Holds.** The six sources of entropy with their health tests, the mixing, the words and their list,
the master seed, and the ten branches each key of a person is seeded from. All of it drawn and held
on the device. The keys themselves stand on the suite, which arrives two stages later and carries
the last link of the chain with it.

**Done when.** The chain from entropy through the words, the stretch and the branch a record signs
from reproduces value for value, a source that is not alive contributes neither a length nor bytes,
a bad draw is refused rather than warned about, and the gate refuses a set whose fingerprint of the
list, salt, count of iterations, branch domain or branch length has moved.

## Stage 5 — value, names, channels, and the objects of consensus

**Holds.** The note, its commitment and nullifier; every nullifier of the set; the slots of a name
and of a channel with their chains, keys, heads and points; the fingerprint; the contact key. Then
every layout with its invariants — proposal, confirmation and its light form, candidacy, the three
objects of a name and of a channel, publication, node of the fold, beacon — the state tables, their
roots, and the Genesis State Hash.

**Done when.** Every vector of those derivations reproduces, the negative vector of a tag fails as
it must, an object of the wrong length is refused before it is parsed, the bytes of a serialized
proposal and of a serialized frame reproduce their frozen digests, the digest of the parameters the
Decree publishes equals the one the set holds, and the door that computes the Genesis State Hash
answers with nothing while a parameter of the Decree stands unpublished — that value lands with the
stage that publishes the parameter and is checked there. The keys the two spaces of slots publish
stand on the suite and arrive with it; what this stage freezes of them is the seed each is
generated from.

## Stage 6 — the cryptographic suite

**Holds.** ML-DSA-65 in its deterministic form, ML-KEM-768, ChaCha20-Poly1305, and the commitment
that carries standing over its lattice with its matrix expanded from the seed.

**Done when.** Published known-answer vectors pass, two independent implementations of each agree
byte for byte, the three frozen vectors of the standing commitment reproduce, the fold of two
commitments yields the frozen node rather than the sum of its children, and the last link of the
chain of a birth — the key a record answers with, from the branch the stage before it derived —
reproduces under the gate.

## Stage 7 — the wire

**Holds.** The cell with both seals, the erasure code with its field and matrix, the unit of a link
with its framing and reassembly, and the four flights of the handshake.

**Done when.** The vectors of the cell, the parity and the handshake reproduce; a group missing four
cells reconstructs; a unit of the wrong length is discarded in silence; two processes exchange
cells through a third that carries them and learns nothing.

## Stage 8 — the pulse without proofs

**Holds.** Eligibility, the ticket, the aggregate, both canonical orders, the holder of a tag, the
beacon and its identifier, the round nullifier, both retargets, the floor of offered slots, the
fold of a window, and the cement.

**Done when.** Every retarget vector reproduces bit for bit under integer arithmetic, and a
simulated population of machines closes windows at the derived count of rounds with the cement
recomputed by every one of them.

## Stage 9 — the layers of the set, and the doors before the circuits

**Holds.** The layer every value belongs to, read off the block it stands in rather than marked on
it or remembered: the grammar that has no replacement, the doors that name a function, a suite and
a form, the parameters the Decree binds, and the planes that publish only the vocabulary. With them
the three tables and their first rows, the one byte-level split of the Decree into what a boundary
may move and what it may not, the law that a class names one shape, the one declaration the
registry is written from and the one each layout is written and read from, and the gate grown to
refuse a value standing in no block or in two, a constant of the library standing in neither, a
layout publishing a seventh kind, a table read by count rather than by name, and a heading of a
derivation disagreeing with the row it restates.

**Why it stands here and not later.** Two dependencies, neither of them a preference. The circuit
of the stage after this one binds the compression of the hash inside itself, so it must be written
already knowing that the function is a row of a table and not a constant — a door added after the
circuit is the circuit rebuilt. And the split of the Decree is free only while the Genesis State
Hash does not exist; it does not, because one parameter stands unpublished, and it costs a fork the
day the twelfth stage publishes that parameter.

**What it has to leave standing.** The health of the Kernel is one property — the map from an
object to its preimage is injective — and every rule of the grammar reduces to it. Six things carry
that property, and this stage is done only when each is held by a compiler or by the gate rather
than by care:

- the alphabet of a domain, so that the NUL after a name separates — held by the type;
- the registry of the names themselves, none of them repeated and none of them minted outside it —
  held by one declaration;
- the scope an identifier is taken over — held by the type;
- the widths a preimage is concatenated from, the body of one value included — held by the type;
- the shape an identifier belongs to, so that a preimage names its object and not merely its plane —
  held by the registry, one domain to one shape;
- the agreement of the writing and the reading of every layout — held by one declaration both derive
  from.

A seventh stands beside them and is of the set rather than of this tree: that every value has one
place, which placement settles and the gate reads from both sides.

**Who owns what, since much of it is not this tree's to do.** Thirty of the forty-three change
the normative set, and this plan is subordinate to that set rather than a driver of it: they travel
the lifecycle of a change the Constitution states, as one proposal — written and standing as
`MIP-2`, which supersedes the proposal before it — and they are listed here as the condition this
stage waits on, not as work it performs. Thirteen are this tree's — six that harden the Kernel
where it is written, and seven that grow the gate — and they are marked so. A stage that commanded
the set would invert the one relation the whole arrangement rests on.

**In what order, and which of them is dated.** The split of the Decree goes first, and with it the
two things that share its day: the width of a hash output, which becomes a row of the parameters
and therefore enters the block the Genesis State Hash binds, and the empty value of both
constructions, which moves the roots that hash binds. Both cost nothing while that hash cannot be
computed, and a fork once it can. One item carries a later date of its own: the class that names
one shape is free while no vector freezes an identifier of the two planes, and none does today.
Nothing else in this stage has a date at all. The contradictions follow, because the set locks on
an open finding and nothing else is accepted while one stands. The layers as text come third, this
tree's thirteen fourth — the six that harden the Kernel before the seven that grow the gate, since
the gate reads blocks and registers that do not exist earlier and the hardening settles an
interface the gate then reads once — and what follows from the whole, last. Within the first group
the relation of a population to a path is settled before any row moves, since the answer decides
which rows there are.

**Done when.** Every value of the set stands in exactly one block and the gate says so from both
sides — the blocks for what stands twice, the register of the library for what stands nowhere; the
three tables, the split, and the width of a hash output among the parameters stand; every class
domain names one shape; the writing and the reading of every layout come from one declaration;
every contradiction named below is closed in it; this tree's thirteen stand — a wrong grammar
refused by the compiler and each case of the gate refused by name; and every existing object
verifies bit-exactly before and after — what moves is what the two dated items move, the block of
parameters and the empty roots, and no layout of any object gains or loses a byte.

*Of the names and the place — of the set, third wave:*

1. The word `tier` stays with the four passes over the set; the layers take names of their own —
   the Kernel, the doors, the Decree, the planes — and are never numbered as passes are.
2. The designation of the Kernel enters the Constitution as an extension of the list, by pointer,
   a divergence between the table and a document resolved in favour of the document.
3. The test of membership is written so a reader decides it rather than an authority: name a
   replacement — named, and it is a door; does it stand as bytes on the wire — it does, and it is
   a value of a table; would two implementations differing here produce different bytes — they
   would not, and it is not of the core at all. The Kernel is what survives all three, and
   nothing enters it by assertion.
4. The law of the Kernel and its one carve are written: a plane publishes of any act only the
   vocabulary, and the deliberate publication of a participant is the exception, marked in every
   ledger it appears in.

*Of Canon — of the set, third wave:*

5. The Kernel holds no value at all, and therefore nothing of it is marked by hand. Every number
   and every function of the set stands as a row of one block — the parameters of the Decree, its
   forms, the suite table, the hash table, the form table — or as a frozen vector, which is an
   artifact of verification and belongs to no layer. The layer of a value is then the block it
   stands in: read off rather than assigned, and a value standing in no block, or in two, is a
   defect. What a mark would have left to judgement, placement settles — and the rule takes no
   exception: a quantity the Kernel *demands* is not thereby a value *of* the Kernel. It stands in
   the block that binds it, and the Kernel demands it from there.
6. The table of sizes is dissolved into the blocks, since a table of loose values is exactly what
   item 5 forbids: the sizes of a scheme to the row of its suite, the widths of a label, a seal and
   a nonce to the form table, and the width of a hash output to the parameters of the Decree. The
   last of the four is not where a reader would first put it, and the reason is the reason for the
   whole split: that width is the width of every root the Genesis State Hash binds, of every
   identifier, of every nullifier and of every commitment, so a boundary able to move it would open
   a chain that is not this one. It therefore stands where nothing at a boundary reaches, and the
   hash table demands it rather than declaring it. What retires at a boundary is the function and
   never its width, which is the price of the ninth of the twelve properties and is named here
   rather than left to be discovered: a chain that needs another width is another chain. The set
   already stands on that number where it says what Grover leaves of it, so this moves the number
   and not the decision. Being a row of the parameters, it enters the block that hash binds, and
   so it carries the same day as the split itself.
7. The section of the hash primitive parts into the form and the function — the shape with its
   NUL to the Kernel, the present function to the first row of the hash table — and the section
   of the form names no function and no width.
8. Canon gains the table of demands: for each form of the Kernel, the property it asks of the
   function — collision, preimage, pseudorandomness, width, and provability inside the circuit —
   so that admission of a row becomes a check and not an argument. The width is asked against the
   row of the parameters that holds it, so a function answering at another width is refused rather
   than admitted with a note beside it.
9. The alphabet of a domain name is normative, and the sentence beside it states that freedom
   from prefixes follows from the alphabet rather than from a walk of pairs.
10. The rule of fixed widths is normative: parts are one concatenation only under it, and a part
    of variable length carrying no length of its own is refused. It holds for a preimage of one
    part exactly as for many — a body assembled from several values carries their lengths inside
    it, and a body that is one value is the whole of the preimage and needs none.
11. A class domain names one shape and never a plane holding several. The identifier of an object
    is taken under the domain of that object, so its shape is recoverable from the preimage rather
    than inferred from its length; two shapes under one domain is a defect of the Kernel, because
    two objects whose canonical bytes coincide would carry one identifier and no rule of the form
    would say they differ. Today one domain stands over the three objects of a name and one over
    the objects of a channel, and what separates them is that their widths happen to differ. The
    registry therefore gains six rows in place of the two, one for every object of either space,
    since the two spaces share the shape of a commitment and of a renewal and a shared shape under
    a shared domain would give one identifier to two objects. The price is those rows and nothing
    else: no byte of any object moves, and no frozen vector moves, since nothing computes those
    identifiers yet and no vector holds one. That is also what dates it — the day a vector
    freezes one, the row costs a re-freezing.

12. A row of the registry names the shape of the preimage its domain opens: a body of one value,
    or parts of stated widths. The set describes that shape today in the prose beside every
    derivation, and a column moves it to where the door can read it — after which a caller who
    lays two values beside each other and offers them as one body is refused by the door under a
    domain that names parts, which is the one hold of the hand item 32 cannot close from inside
    the builder. It is one decision at two owners: the set writes the column, this tree makes the
    doors read it, and the gate compares the column with the shapes the tree declares. It moves no
    byte: a shape is what the set already says, written where it can be checked.

*Of the Decree — of the set, first wave, and the only one with a day it stops being free:*

13. The Decree parts into two named blocks — the parameters and the forms — each with its own
    order of encoding.
14. What the Genesis State Hash binds is stated: the block of parameters, since a boundary that
    moves a form must leave it untouched. What binds each form is stated beside it, and the two
    answers differ, which is why silence here would be the overstatement this set refuses
    everywhere else. The width of a hash output stands among the parameters for the same reason
    the five roots do: every one of those roots is of that width, and a block binding a root while
    leaving its width to a boundary would bind nothing. A form the circuits prove over — the depth
    of the note tree, the shape of a frame — stays bound through the artifact the Decree binds by
    hash, since a circuit built to another shape is another artifact and another hash. A form the
    circuits never touch — the size of the cell, the group of the erasure code, the reach of a
    path, the width of a name field — leaves the binding of the chain altogether, and that is the
    price of the door rather than an oversight: today a machine holding the wrong one fails the
    genesis hash and never joins, and after the split it joins and fails when it first speaks. What
    holds two machines to one size is then the row of the form table and the span of a drain,
    checked on the wire and not at the join, and the ledger of this stage says so where a reader
    will look.
15. The sentence that these numbers cannot be changed is narrowed to the block it is true of.
16. The rows that decide a width move to the forms: `cell_bytes`, `erasure_group`, `redundancy`,
    `note_tree_depth`, `name_max_length`, and the three of a frame with the count they derive.
    They are every row of the Decree the tree reads for a width save one, and the one is the width
    of a hash output, which arrives among the parameters by item 6 and stays there. Which rows
    those are is checked by reading rather than recalled.
17. The split is closed under derivation: no parameter is derived from a form. One relation
    would have broken that — `max_machines = 2^(path_max - hop_min)` — and it is settled before a
    row moves, because the answer decides which rows there are. It is settled by removing the
    bound: no row of the Decree counts machines, and none may be added, so there is no parameter
    on the near side of that relation at all. `path_max` is therefore a row of the forms, where a
    boundary widens it at the price written beside it, and what it bounds is the reach of one
    delivery rather than the population of a network. The two other ways out are recorded and
    refused: severing the derivation lets the two disagree, and keeping a population bound while
    the reach that feeds it moves at a boundary of the wire moves a bound of consensus with it.

*Of the doors — of the set, third wave:*

18. The hash table stands, its first row the present function; a row names a function alone,
    because the width stands among the parameters and the table admits only functions answering
    at it.
19. The form table stands, its first row the present layouts; a row binds the layouts of the
    public objects, of the deposits at points, and the size of the cell.
20. The suite table is marked a door, and the sizes of a scheme follow its row rather than a
    section of their own.
21. The three quantities of a boundary enter Canon: the distance a boundary stands at, the span
    of a drain, and the support a successor era owes the era it closes. Each arrives with the
    derivation the set asks of a constant — the quantity it is measured against, and the reasoning
    from that quantity to the number — since three numbers admitted by assertion would be three
    doors of exactly the kind these layers are drawn to close.
22. The rule that resolves an era from a window is written, and beside it the sentence that no
    object carries one.

*Of the contradictions — of the set, second wave:*

23. One reading of the identifier of a publication of a channel stands — the rule of a signed
    object, or the sentence of the section that describes it, and not both. What that identifier
    is taken *under* is settled by item 11 and is not this question; what remains is which
    sentence states it.
24. `protocol_version` is named for what it is — the major and the minor of the grammar, carrying
    no era — and the rule that reads it says so where a device judges a proposal. The alternative
    this line once offered, that the field leaves the header, is refused rather than left open: a
    joining device reads the version rule off the newest proposal before it trusts anything else,
    and a header four bytes shorter is a proposal of another length, another frozen digest and
    another day.
25. `max_summands` is named for what it measures, and the count a sum truly opens for is stated
    beside it.
26. The empty value of both constructions is separated by domain rather than left at thirty-two
    zeros, so that what a root says about absence stands on the form and not on the function. Two
    things lean on those zeros today and they are one lean: the injection of a count of leaves
    into an append-only root, and the proof of absence of the sparse construction, which folds
    thirty-two zeros where a leaf would stand and thereby says that a key holds nothing. A leaf
    hashing to zero would make a nullifier that was spent provably unspent. It is one decision at
    two owners: the set states it here and this tree writes it at item 31, and the two move
    together or neither does.
27. The length of a proof is a declaration of its artifact, and every total it enters is stated
    as a quantity of an era.
28. The proposal is brought to the set it requires; the phrase by which a row of the hash table
    names a function and its width is narrowed to the function, the width being the row of the
    parameters item 6 places; and every quantity the proposal cites as fixed is fixed before its
    conformance set is claimed by anyone.

*Of the Kernel where it is written — of this tree, fourth wave, before the gate:*

29. The alphabet of a domain moves from a test into the type: a `const fn` validates it, and a
    name carrying a byte outside it fails to compile rather than fails a test. The separation of
    every domain rests on this one property, since the NUL separates because no name of the
    registry can hold one.
30. The identifier narrows to a signed scope: the door taking a class and any bytes at all becomes
    of the crate, and the only way to the identifier of an object is through the scope that object
    states. This is the door through which the set came to hold two readings of one rule, and a
    narrowed door would have carried the question to the type.
31. The empty value takes a domain of its own instead of thirty-two zeros, in both constructions
    and in the walk that verifies against them, so that a count of leaves enters an append-only
    root, and an absence enters a sparse one, by the form rather than by no leaf hashing to zero.
    The two constructions compute their empties through one function, so it is one line for both —
    and it is one line for both only because the class was carried across the second tree instead
    of being closed at the first. This one goes in the first wave and not the fourth, with the
    split of the Decree and for the same reason: the empty value moves every empty internal, every
    empty root and therefore the five roots the Genesis State Hash binds, so it is free until the
    day that hash can be computed and a fork after it. It is the decision of item 26, written where
    it is written.
32. The preimage is taken by a builder of widths, and the door that takes raw bytes closes behind
    it. A part of a width the set fixes — by the type that carries it, or by a row the Decree binds
    — is admitted as it is; a value of no fixed width is admitted only with its length before it,
    written by the builder and not by the caller; and a body that is one value passes a door of its
    own, where there is nothing to split from it. The first of the three stands already: a part is
    built from a reference to an array and from nothing else, and four places where a signature had
    erased a width were given it back. What remains is the other two, and they are the half that
    matters: while a door takes raw bytes of any length, the injectivity of a preimage is held by
    whoever writes the call. The three places that assemble a body by hand pass through the builder
    — the parameters and the five roots under the Genesis State Hash, the run of identities of an

## Stage 11 — the circuit of a frame

**Holds.** The gadgets — the compression of the hash, the path of a tree, the derivation of a note,
of a commitment and of a nullifier, the disjunction of a note and a right, the balance, the bound on
the rate — and the constraint set of a frame assembled from them, with the prover that fills its
trace.

**Done when.** A real frame is proven and verified, a frame that spends nothing it holds fails to
prove, the length equals the one the set derives, and the peak memory of proving on the weakest

derivation, closed there and not by moving the device.

### What the ground under the gadgets turned out to be

Two properties of the shape had to be established before a single gadget was worth writing, and
both are now held by construction rather than by intention.

**The selector's period is the block and never the trace.** A transition of a gadget holds inside
a block and not across the seam to the next, where a fresh compression starts. A column of period
`rows_per_compression` that is zero at the last index switches the transition off at every seam,
and the row where the trace wraps is the last row of the last block — so the wrap costs nothing of
its own. The column is held as `Q(x^(n/p))` with `p` coefficients, so a prover transforms two
thousand values and a verifier evaluates two thousand coefficients where the length of the trace
would have asked a million of each. Sixteen kilobytes of description per selector, paid once.

**Every constraint of the circuit is of degree two.** The composition of a constraint of degree `d`
carries degree about `(d − 1)·n`, and the folding carries it down by exactly what carries `n` down
to the tail — so the tail admits `d ≤ 2` at every height. This is not a limitation of what a
circuit may say: the choice of three bits is already of degree two, the majority is `t + c·(a + b −
2t)` with `t = a·b`, and the exclusive or of three is two of two. It is a limitation on how, and it
is paid in columns.

### The shape of the circuit, derived

What follows is the construction the gadgets are written against, so that the work builds rather
than derives again.

**The tree of notes is walked in the circuit's own hash.** What stood here before this pass —
that the frame is 261 compressions of SHA-256 and the heavy engineering follows — fell to one
question of the author's about strain: the paths of the tree of notes are witnesses of proofs and
of nothing else, so the tree folds under the proof hash, and 240 of the 261 compressions become
246 permutations at thirty-two rows each. The trace of a frame is 2¹⁶; twenty-one compressions of
SHA-256 remain — the nullifiers and commitments, where hiding lives — and the forward-shift
engineering below now serves them with threefold headroom instead of carrying nine tenths of the
circuit.

**The frame is one gadget repeated.** Every part of it — the path of a redemption, its nullifier,
its commitment, the outputs of a spend, the rate nullifier — is compressions of the hash. Two
hundred and sixty-one of them, at `rows_per_compression` each, is the whole of the trace. The
circuit is therefore the compression, plus the wiring that says which output feeds which input,
plus the arithmetic of the balance.

**A rotation is a reweighting and not a row offset.** A factor reaches a row forward and never
back, so the bit at `(j + 6) mod 32` is out of reach at row `j` near the end of a block. It is not
needed: a rotated word is the same bits accumulated under different weights, and the weights are a
periodic column of the block. A column holding the rotated bits is tied to the column holding the
bits by one comparison of two accumulations — and since both columns are bits and the weights are
distinct powers of two, equal accumulations force equal bits. Every rotation of `Σ0`, `Σ1`, `σ0`
and `σ1` is written this way, and no row offset is used for any of them.

**The state is carried in columns and never reached for across rows.** A shift names this row or
the one after it and no other — the slot of the out-of-domain evaluations holds a column at the
point and at the point one row on, so a factor at a further row is a value no proof carries, and
the format refuses one where a description becomes a description. What the round function asks for
— `b` being `a` one round back and `c` two rounds back — is therefore held in columns of their own,
carried unchanged across the rows of a round by a transition the block's own selector switches, and
read at the row where they are needed. What that costs is columns, which is what the bound on the
width already counts, and it is the same price the set names for every constraint of higher degree.

**The columns, counted.** The bits of `a` and `e` and of the four past values the round function
reads bitwise; the rotated copies each rotation needs; two or three accumulators, which carry the
sums of the round and the comparisons that tie the rotations. The count lands at the bound on the
width, which is what the bound is for.

- **The balance** — an amount read as one number below two to the sixty-second rather than four

## The admission plane, settled

Sybil resistance is one mechanism and it is the invitation. A machine or a record enters only by
redeeming an invitation; an invitation is issued by a member at a rate its own
witnessed-and-anchored continuity fixes — witnessed meaning woven into the cemented fabric, not
asserted alone, so a farm that witnesses only itself is an island and issues nothing. The two
halves close each other: without the rate one corrupted witness admits a multitude; without the
anchor a farm matures itself into an issuer. Together, admission costs one corrupted
fabric-connected person per rate of newcomers, and nothing cheaper — not compute, not money, not
wall-clock. The reward is not touched: the share of a window stays equal, because to pay for
usefulness the set would have to measure who served, and that measurement is the graph every plane
refuses.

What the circuits must additionally assert, beyond the frame and the window above:

- **Issuing an invitation.** The issuer's witnessed-and-anchored continuity clears the threshold —
  its fabric of time reaches the cemented set within the reach the Decree fixes, proven and not
  disclosed; its index of the period is below the rate that continuity earns; and the nullifier of
  issuance for that period and index is spent. What is published is one commitment — the leaf of
  the invite tree — and one nullifier, and nothing of the issuer.
- **Redeeming an invitation.** A candidacy or a record-opening proves a leaf of a live period's
  invite tree and knowledge of the token behind it, disclosing neither which leaf nor who issued
  it, and spends the nullifier of that redemption once.
- **The reach of a witness** is a new gadget beside the path of a tree and the derivation of a
  nullifier: that a leaf of the fabric traces to the cemented set within a bounded depth.

Its derivations are settled and checked against an independent oracle, and they freeze with the
circuit that enforces them: the branch `mt-invite-key`, the token key `mt-invite-pk`, the leaf
`mt-invite-cm`, the nullifiers `mt-invite-redeem-nf` and `mt-invite-issue-nf`, and the append-only
invite tree that lives a period. The rate a continuity earns is `max(0, witnessed_segments −
continuity_required_segments + 1)`, at most five, and it reads only numbers the Decree already
holds, so it adds no constant of its own. One constant stays open, and it is derived rather than
pinned: the reach a witness must have to the cemented fabric, which is the diameter of the living.
Nothing here publishes more than commitment, nullifier and proof — the vocabulary and no seventh
kind — so the plane extends this network and is not another.
